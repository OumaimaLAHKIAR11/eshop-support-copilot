STORE POLICY — REFUNDS
Store name: NovaShop (fictional store)
Version: 1.0

1. A customer may request a refund for an eligible returned item after the return is received and inspected.
2. Once a refund is approved, it is sent to the original payment method.
3. Banks and payment providers may take 5–10 business days to display an issued refund.
4. Support must not promise that a refund has been approved or issued unless verified order data confirms it.
5. If a customer says a refund was approved but it has not appeared after 10 business days, ask for the order number and escalate the case to the billing team.
6. Shipping fees are not automatically refundable. Eligibility depends on the order circumstances and must be reviewed by support.
7. Items marked “final sale” are not eligible for a change-of-mind refund, except where applicable law requires otherwise.
8. If the information provided is insufficient to determine eligibility, explain what is missing and refer the case to a human support agent.
