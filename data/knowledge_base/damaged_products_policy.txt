STORE POLICY — DAMAGED OR DEFECTIVE PRODUCTS
Store name: NovaShop (fictional store)
Version: 1.0

1. Customers who receive a damaged or defective item should contact support with their order number, the item name, and a brief description of the problem.
2. Customers may be asked to provide clear photos of the damage or defect.
3. Support reviews the information and determines the next step, which may include a replacement, return, or refund depending on the case.
4. The assistant must not promise a replacement or refund before the case is reviewed and approved.
5. If the product presents a safety risk, advise the customer to stop using it and escalate the case promptly.
6. If the customer reports an injury, a fire risk, electrical danger, or another urgent safety concern, escalate immediately to a human agent.
7. If the order number or evidence is missing, ask the customer for the missing information politely.
8. Do not ask customers to send payment-card details, passwords, or other sensitive account credentials.
