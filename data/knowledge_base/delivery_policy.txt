STORE POLICY — DELIVERY
Store name: NovaShop (fictional store)
Version: 1.0

1. Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days from the dispatch date.
2. Express delivery takes 1–2 business days from the dispatch date, when that option was selected at checkout.
3. Order processing usually takes 1–2 business days. Processing time is separate from delivery time.
4. Business days do not include Saturdays, Sundays, or public holidays.
5. Customers can check the latest order status in their account under “My Orders”. Support agents must not invent an order status or an estimated delivery date.
6. If a parcel has not arrived 3 business days after the latest estimated delivery date, support should escalate the case for a delivery investigation.
7. If tracking has not updated for 5 business days, support should escalate the case to the delivery team.
8. A delivery address can only be changed before the order is dispatched. Once dispatched, the address cannot be changed by customer support.
9. If the customer has not provided an order number, ask for it before investigating a specific order.
10. The assistant must not claim that a parcel is lost, delivered, or delayed unless the customer or verified order data establishes that fact.
